UltraEdge ("the app") is an Android companion for EdgeTX radios. This policy explains what the app does
and does not do with your data.

## Data we collect
**None.** UltraEdge does not collect, store off your device, or share any personal data. There are no
user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking or third-party SDKs of that kind.

## How the app works
UltraEdge communicates **only with the radio you physically connect it to, over the USB cable**. It reads
and edits your radio's configuration, shows telemetry, and can copy flight-log files from the radio's SD
card to your phone at your request. All of this happens locally between the phone and the radio. The app
does not require or request internet access.

## Files on your device
Configuration backups and flight logs you choose to pull are stored in the app's own storage on your
phone. You can share or delete them yourself from within the app. They are not sent anywhere by the app.

## Future optional features
A future update may add optional cloud features (for example, backing up flight logs to your own Google
Drive). If added, they will be **opt-in**: the app will clearly ask for your permission before anything
leaves your device, and will only ever transfer the specific files you choose. This policy will be
updated before any such feature ships.

## Permissions
UltraEdge uses USB host access to talk to the radio. It does not request location, contacts, camera,
microphone, or other sensitive permissions.

## Children
UltraEdge is a tool for radio-control hobbyists and is not directed at children.

## Contact
Questions or requests: open an issue at `https://github.com/50UR4V/UltraEdge-app/issues`
_(or the support email listed on the Google Play page)_.
